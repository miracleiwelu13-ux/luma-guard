LumaGuard Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026. Applies to the LumaGuard Android app.

The short version. LumaGuard checks the names of websites on your phone and stops adult ones. We do not keep a record of the sites you visit. We store only what is needed for your account and your streak. There are no ads, no analytics, and we do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

LumaGuard is an Android app that blocks adult websites and supports people who want to quit porn. In this policy, "LumaGuard", "we" and "us" mean the people who run LumaGuard. You can reach us at hello@lumaguard.app.

2. How the blocker works

LumaGuard uses Android's VPN feature to create a local VPN on your phone. It is not a connection to a VPN company. Only website name lookups (DNS) are routed through it, and the rest of your internet traffic is not touched.

  • Lookups for adult websites are stopped on your phone, using a block list and a short keyword list stored in the app.
  • All other lookups are forwarded to Cloudflare's family DNS service (1.1.1.3 and 1.0.0.3), which also filters adult sites. Cloudflare handles them under its own privacy policy.
  • LumaGuard does not store, log or share these lookups, and does not use them for advertising.
  • Currently the forwarded lookups are not encrypted. We plan to move them to an encrypted connection.

3. What stays on your device

  • The website lookups described above.
  • Whether protection is on, and the timers for the 1-hour cooldown.
  • The dates on which a site was blocked, until they have been sent to your streak (section 4).

5. The AI companion

When you use the chat on the urge screen, the text you type (the last few messages) is sent to our server and forwarded to Groq, our AI provider, which writes the reply. We do not store your chat messages. Groq processes them under its own privacy terms. The AI companion is not a therapist or a medical service. Please do not share information you want kept private, and contact a qualified professional or emergency services if you are in crisis.

7. Who else handles your data

We use these service providers to run LumaGuard. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, login, and server functions.
  • Groq: generating AI companion replies.
  • Resend: sending login confirmation and password reset emails.
  • Cloudflare: family DNS filtering (section 2), our website, and our domain name.

We do not sell your personal data, share it for advertising, or use it for anything unrelated to running LumaGuard. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. We do not store chat messages. Daily message counters are kept only to enforce the daily limit.

9. Your choices and deleting your account

  • Delete your account in the app: on the home screen, tap Delete account and type DELETE. Your account, streak and chat usage are deleted permanently.
  • If you cannot open the app, see lumaguard.app/delete-account or email hello@lumaguard.app from the address on your account. We will respond within 30 days.
  • To access, correct or export your data, email us at the same address.
  • You can turn off protection and the uninstall block in the app. Both use a passage and a 1-hour cooldown by design, to help you avoid acting on impulse.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device. Account data on our servers stays until you delete your account.

10. Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted. Access to our database is restricted by account-level security rules, and API keys for our providers are kept on our server, never in the app. As noted in section 2, forwarded website lookups are not yet encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

LumaGuard is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, such as new features that use your data in new ways, we will also notify users in the app or by email.
